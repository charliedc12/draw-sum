# CURRICULUM

Six phases. Daily steps are 5-10 min. Weekend units are 30-60 min sessions.
Units repeat: cycle their steps until requiredReps is met.

## PHASE 1 — Line control  (maxWeeks 5)

Unit 1.1 Marks (daily, requiredReps 8)
  1 Straight lines, dot to dot, 20 reps. 6 min. Pen. Look at the endpoint, not the pen tip.
    Failure: hooking the line at the far end because your eye followed the tip.
  2 Superimposed lines — one line, trace it 5 times without drifting. 6 min. Pen.
    Failure: fraying at the far end. The start is always tighter than the finish.
  3 Ghosted lines — hover the motion twice, commit once. 15 lines. 7 min. Pen.
  4 Long single-stroke curves, 15 reps. 7 min. Pen. Failure: sketchy repair strokes.

Unit 1.2 Ellipses (daily, requiredReps 6)
  1 Ellipses in a shallow angled box, fill the row. 7 min. Pen.
  2 Ellipses in a steep box, fill the row. 7 min. Pen.
  3 Ellipse funnel — a curved corridor, ellipses filling it, all minor axes aligned to
    the centre line. 8 min. Pen. Failure: each ellipse tilted independently.

Unit 1.3 Boxes (daily, requiredReps 6)
  1 Eight freehand boxes, rotated at random. 8 min. Pen, no rulers ever.
  2 Six boxes with every edge drawn twice. 8 min. Pen.
  3 Six boxes, each larger than the last, largest filling the page. 8 min. Pen.

Unit 1.W Weekend contour (weekend, requiredReps 4)
  1 Contour studies of 5-10 household objects. One continuous line, no lifting, no
    erasing. 40 min.
  2 One blind contour — eyes on the object only, never on the page. 20 min.
    It will look terrible. That is the drill working.

Subjects (Phase 1): crumpled receipt / open scissors / keys on a ring / mug with the
handle at three-quarter / tangled charging cable / half-peeled banana / wire whisk /
folded towel / sneaker with loose laces / celery bunch / clothespin / adjustable wrench

Gate statements (Phase 1):
  - My lines land roughly where I aimed about 7 times in 10.
  - My ellipses are smooth and closed, even when they're the wrong shape.
  - I can draw a box that reads as a box in space without thinking hard.
  - I've done at least 4 weekend contour sessions.

## PHASE 2 — Observation  (maxWeeks 10)

Unit 2.1 Angles (daily, requiredReps 6)
  1 One object, its 5-8 major angles only, as straight lines. Nothing else. 6 min.
  2 Same, but hold your pencil up to compare each real angle before committing. 7 min.
    Failure: you will flatten the extremes. Steep angles get drawn shallower than they are.
  3 Angle-only sketch of something with no right angles — a plant, a shoe. 8 min.

Unit 2.2 Negative space (daily, requiredReps 6)
  1 Draw only the gaps around a chair. 8 min.
  2 Draw only the gaps between the leaves of a houseplant. 8 min.
  3 Draw only the gaps in a bicycle's frame and wheels. 9 min.

Unit 2.3 Measuring (daily, requiredReps 8)
  1 Sketch an object, then measure it against the real thing with a pencil at arm's
    length. Note the direction of your error. 8 min.
  2 Two-value block-in: silhouette plus one internal shadow shape. 5 min.
  3 Comparative proportion — two objects of different size, relative scale correct. 9 min.
    Failure: drawings drift too large toward the bottom of the page.

Unit 2.W Weekend studies (weekend, requiredReps 8)
  1 Photo study, then a self-check pass: hold your drawing beside the reference and
    name the single biggest error out loud. 45 min.
  2 One complexity study, 30 min minimum, from life.

Subjects (Phase 2): chair at an oblique angle / open laptop / stepladder / bicycle
side-on / half-collapsed folding table / staircase railing / radiator / bookshelf
corner / pile of unfolded laundry / monstera or spider plant / kitchen sink with
dishes in it / bare branch with leaves attached

From-life prompts (use anywhere a step calls for fromLife; specificity is the feature):
the third item from the left on your kitchen counter / your own shoe from behind, laces
toward you / the nearest chair, standing and looking down at it / whatever is currently
in your sink / the corner where two walls meet the ceiling / the car parked furthest
from your door, rear three-quarter / your non-drawing hand gripping a doorknob / the
view out any window, but only shapes above the horizon line

Gate statements (Phase 2):
  - I can block in overall proportions in the first 90 seconds and not need to shift them.
  - When I compare a drawing to its reference, my errors are small and scattered rather
    than the same distortion every time.
  - I've done at least 4 complexity studies of 25 minutes or longer.
